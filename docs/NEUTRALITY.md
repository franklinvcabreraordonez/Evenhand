# Neutrality charter (draft v0.1)

Evenhand only has value if the parties to a dispute can rely on it as a neutral record. These are the commitments the project is built around. They apply to the code in this repository and to any hosted service built from it.

1. **The witness sits outside the agent's trust boundary.** A record signed only by the agent is self-serving. Witness keys are never held by the agent or its operator.
2. **The verifier is open and runs offline.** Anyone can check a receipt or an evidence packet without trusting us or contacting a server.
3. **No custody.** We do not hold or move customer funds.
4. **No conflicted products.** We do not sell guarantees, insurance or any product whose payout depends on our verdicts until the legal, capital and governance work is done and published here first.
5. **No consumer scoring.** We do not build reputation or credit scores about individual people.
6. **Humans decide adverse consumer outcomes.** Verdicts are recommendations. An adverse outcome for a consumer needs a human reviewer and an appeal path.
7. **Open rules, public changes.** The adjudication checks and thresholds live in this repository. Changes are made in public with a change log.
8. **Disclosure.** We will publish material funding, partnerships and conflicts of interest that could affect neutrality.
9. **Interoperate, don't capture.** We verify open standards (Verifiable Intent, AP2) rather than define a proprietary mandate format.

This charter is a draft written by the maintainer before any company exists. It will be revised with input from builders, merchants, issuers and insurers.
