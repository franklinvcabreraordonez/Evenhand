A neutral witness for AI agents that spend money.

When an agent buys the wrong thing, nobody can prove what the user allowed. Evenhand records what the agent actually did, checks it against the user’s signed permission, and gives merchants, banks and insurers a verdict they can act on.

Agent purchases have no neutral record.

        
As agents start to spend money for people, disputes get hard to settle, and every party’s evidence is partial.

        

          

            
The log belongs to the agent

            
The record of what happened is often signed by the agent itself, so it proves little to anyone else.

          

          

            
Merchants carry the loss

            
Merchants absorb the cost by default, and banks respond by restricting outside agents altogether.

          

          

            
Nobody has said who pays

            
Card networks cover cards only and have not said who is responsible when an agent overreaches.

          

        

      

    


    

      

        
How Evenhand works

        
Three parts, each independently checkable.

        

          

            1
            
The user signs a mandate

            
What the agent may buy, from which merchants, for how much, and until when.

          

          

            2
            
A witness records the actions

            
Receipts are signed outside the agent’s control and chained together, so edits, deletions and reordering show up.

          

          

            3
            
Evenhand returns a verdict

            
Every check that ran is listed in an evidence packet a merchant, bank or insurer can verify offline.

Who it is for

        
Evenhand is built for the people who have to settle these disputes.

        

          

            
Teams that let agents spend

            
Agent wallets, card programs and shopping agents that need evidence a counterparty will accept.

          

          

            
Merchants and issuers

            
Risk and dispute teams that need to tell a valid agent purchase from an unauthorized one.

          

          

            
Insurers and auditors

            
Anyone pricing or reviewing agent risk who needs a verifiable record instead of a screenshot.

          

        

      

    


    

      

        
Where things stand

        

          
Version 0.1, an open-source prototype. It runs on synthetic data, has not been audited, and is not for real money or customer data.

          
The mandate format is a simplified one. Adapters for open agent-payment standards (Verifiable Intent and AP2) are planned and not yet built, and there is no hosted service yet.

          
The design commitments, including no custody of funds and no consumer scoring, are in the neutrality charter.
